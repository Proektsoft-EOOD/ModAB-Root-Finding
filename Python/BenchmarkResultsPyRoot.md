Detailed Benchmark Results - PyRoot
===
The modAB algorithm is benchmarked against the bracketed root-finding methods of [PyRoot](https://github.com/SimpleArt/pyroot) by Jack Nguyen, in respect to number of evaluations and execution times:
* `bisect` - Bisection method (`method="bisection"`)
* `secant` - Secant method with bisection safeguard (`method="secant"`)
* `dekker` - Dekker's method (1969) (`method="dekker"`)
* `brent` - Brent's method (1973) (`method="brent"`)
* `chandr` - Chandrupatla's method (1997) (`method="chandrupatla"`)
* `ch_mixed` - Chandrupatla's method mixed with quadratic interpolation, the PyRoot default (`method="chandrupatla mixed"`)
* `modAB` - Modified Anderson Bjork's method (Ganchovski & Traykov, 2023; improved 2026)

All PyRoot methods are called through `pyroot.solver(f, a, b, method=..., x_err=1e-14, r_err=1e-14, refine=0)`.
The final refinement step is disabled (`refine=0`, default is 15), so that PyRoot stops at the same tolerance as modAB.
With the default `refine=15`, PyRoot needs 1991 evaluations in total with `ch_mixed` (vs 1653 with `refine=0`), and fails with `ZeroDivisionError` on f40 (`secant`) and f77 (`dekker`).
